Every part of the pool, line by line.

One customer's pool needed a week of rebuilding. Another just needed a quick answer. Here is the range of work Jennosa takes on.

Talk it over with Mike
01

New plumbing

Replacing the pipes that move water between the pool and the equipment, the part nobody sees until it fails.

02

Steel stairs

Swapping old stairs for steel stairs built into the pool wall.

03

Cement bottoms

A new cement bottom gives the liner a smooth, solid floor to sit on.

04

Heaters

Adding or replacing a pool heater so the swim season starts sooner and runs longer.

05

Deck jets

Arcs of water from the deck into the pool. They look great and they are fun on a hot day.

06

New liners

The finishing layer that sets the color and look of the whole pool.
